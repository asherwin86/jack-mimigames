New in Block Stacker, Asteroid Blaster, Marble Maze and Simon Cubes
-------------------------------------------------------------------
- Block Stacker: three PERFECT drops in a row win some width back (up to the
  starting width). There is a Streak counter.
- Asteroid Blaster: glowing rocks now and then. Green = mends one hull point,
  cyan = 7 seconds of rapid fire. They do not split; worth 150.
- Marble Maze: four cyan TIME CRYSTALS on the board. Each takes 2 seconds off
  your clock.
- Simon Cubes: every fifth round you must play the sequence BACKWARDS
  (the round announces it).
