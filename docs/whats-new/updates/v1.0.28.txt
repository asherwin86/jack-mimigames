v1.0.28  -  25 Sep 2026
New in Orbit Dodge and Blockcraft
---------------------------------
- Orbit Dodge: GOLD stars count 3. White CHRONO stars slow the debris rings to
  about a third for 5 seconds.
- Blockcraft: seven new blocks - Glowstone (always bright; small pockets deep
  underground), Ice, Red Wool, Blue Wool, Yellow Wool, Green Wool and
  Bookshelf.
- The Blockcraft hotbar now has 18 slots. Slots 12-18 have no number key:
  click them or scroll the mouse wheel (or use the controller bumpers).
- The multiplayer server accepts the new blocks. (An old copy of the app that
  has not updated yet can crash opening a world that contains them - the app
  updates itself when restarted.)
