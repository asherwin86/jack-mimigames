10 new games (32 to 41)
-----------------------
Puzzle games
- Memory Match - flip cards two at a time to find the eight pairs. Every wrong pair
  costs a second. Your score is your time (lower is better).
- Sliding Puzzle - slide the numbered tiles into order, by clicking or with the arrow
  keys. Score is your time.
- Lights Out - pressing a light flips it and its neighbours. Turn them all off,
  then do it again against the clock (each puzzle adds 5 seconds). Score is puzzles cleared.
- Colour Flood - flood the board from the corner one colour at a time before the
  moves run out. Spare moves score bonus points.
- Connect Four - drop discs and get four in a row before the bot does. Every win
  makes the next bot sharper; your score is wins in a row.
Arcade games
- Invader Grid - shoot marching rows of invaders, dodge their bombs, and hit the
  bonus saucer. Waves get faster.
- Pac-Cube - eat every pellet in the maze while three ghosts chase you; big pellets
  let you eat the ghosts.
- Flappy Cube - flap through the gaps between the pipes.
- Gravity Flip - race down a corridor, flipping between floor and ceiling to slip
  past the blocks.
- Catch the Stars - slide a basket under falling stars (gold is worth triple) and
  dodge the bombs.
All ten work with mouse or touch, have on-screen touch buttons where they need them,
and each has its own Rocoin challenges (three targets per game).
