New in Whack-a-Cube, Fruit Slice, Wrecking Ball, Frog Hopper and Artillery Duel
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Whack-a-Cube: white CLOCK cubes add 4 seconds when smashed.
- Fruit Slice: GOLDEN fruit starts a 6-second frenzy (twice the fruit, twice
  the points). Pink HEART fruit gives a life back (only appears when you are
  missing one).
- Wrecking Ball: red TNT blocks. Knock one loose and it blows up the blocks
  around it.
- Frog Hopper: a golden FLY appears on a safe strip when you have lost a life.
  Catch it to get the life back.
- Artillery Duel: some targets SLIDE side to side (worth 50% more) after your
  first few hits. A bullseye earns a spare shell (up to 15).
