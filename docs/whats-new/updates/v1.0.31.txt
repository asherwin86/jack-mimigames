Scroll the game list with a controller
--------------------------------------
- Fixed: with a controller you could not scroll down the list of games. Now the
  right stick scrolls it, and so do the D-pad up/down and the two bumpers
  (LB/RB). Pushing the left-stick cursor to the top or bottom edge of the screen
  also keeps scrolling that way.
- Works in the desktop app and the website with a controller too, and in the
  Android app on the Shield TV.
