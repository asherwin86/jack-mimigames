New in Paddle Rally, Platform Hop, Sumo Arena and Tunnel Run
------------------------------------------------------------
- Paddle Rally: a glowing TARGET moves about the far wall. Bounce the ball off
  it for +2 bonus points; every third hit buys back a lost life. Your score is
  your best rally plus the bonus.
- Platform Hop: cyan SPRINGS on every sixth platform fire you about two floors
  up.
- Sumo Arena: a huge BOSS rolls in every fifth wave. It is heavy and slow to
  turn; shoving it off counts as 3.
- Tunnel Run: fly through the blue ORBS (one in every eighth wall) to slow the
  tunnel to 70% for 4 seconds.
