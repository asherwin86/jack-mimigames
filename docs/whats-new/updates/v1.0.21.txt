New in Cube Dodger, Sky Hoops, Snake Cube and Gem Grab
------------------------------------------------------
- Cube Dodger: SHIELD pickups (cyan ring) soak up one crash and shatter the
  block. STARS add 40 m to your distance.
- Sky Hoops: every seventh ring is a small GOLDEN ring. Harder to thread, but
  it counts as 3 rings and adds 5 seconds.
- Snake Cube: GOLDEN APPLES appear now and then (+3 length, no speed-up, they
  flicker and vanish after 7 seconds). ROCKS start to appear once you have
  eaten 4 apples - hit one and you are out.
- Gem Grab: MAGNET power-up reels in gems for 7 seconds. ICE CRYSTAL freezes
  every drone for 4 seconds (frozen drones cannot hurt you).
