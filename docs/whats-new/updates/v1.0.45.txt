v1.0.45  -  26 Sep 2026
School holiday colours on the menu
----------------------------------
During the school holidays the menu's background sky now gets a new random set of colours every time you open it, and a small "School holidays" greeting appears under the title.
There is a new "Holiday colours" switch in Settings to turn it off (then you get the normal rainbow sky). "Black sky" still overrides it.
The dates used are the Victorian school holidays for 2026 and 2027 (listed in one file, src/engine/Seasons.js, so they are easy to change or extend). To preview it on any day, add ?season=holidays to the address.
Only the menu changes. The games themselves look the same.
