Faster school holiday colours
-----------------------------
The flashing menu colours during the school holidays now change twice a second instead of once (each colour holds for half a second with a very quick fade). It is deliberately kept under three changes a second, which is the usual limit for safe flashing.
Everything else is the same: every palette colour, one flat colour at a time, random order each visit, and the "Holiday colours" switch in Settings turns it off.
