Accounts, cloud worlds and Kart online
--------------------------------------
- Sign in (top of the menu) with a name and password - no email needed.
- Your Blockcraft worlds are saved to your account (up to 8) and appear with a
  cloud badge on any device you sign in on.
- Kart Circuit's Play with Friends and Play Online modes work again, through
  the new account server.
