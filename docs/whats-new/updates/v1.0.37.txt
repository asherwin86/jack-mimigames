v1.0.37  -  25 Sep 2026
9 more new games (42 to 50) - now 50 games
------------------------------------------
Action games
- Tank Battle - drive a tank around a walled arena and wreck waves of enemy tanks
  (heavy ones take three hits). Aim with the mouse or the right stick; repair kits
  drop from some wrecks.
- Horde Survivor - endless swarms close in; you shoot automatically. Collect gems to
  level up (more damage, faster fire, extra shots, speed, magnet, health).
- Rocket Lander - tilt and thrust a rocket onto a small pad: slow, upright and with fuel
  to spare. Each landing gets a smaller pad and stronger gravity.
- Slalom Ski - carve down the mountain through the gates; each gate adds time, missed
  gates and trees cost it.
- Air Hockey - mouse (or stick) mallet against a bot that gets better with every goal
  you score. Concede five and you're out.
Puzzle and strategy games
- Tetra Drop - falling blocks: rotate, slide and hard-drop them to clear rows.
- 2048 Merge - slide the board (arrows, WASD or swipe) and merge equal tiles.
- Parking Panic - squeeze the car into the one free bay against the clock; bumps cost
  time, each park adds it.
- Battleship - find and sink five hidden ships in as few shots as you can (lower is better).
All nine have touch controls where they need them and their own Rocoin challenges.
