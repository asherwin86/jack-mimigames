-----------------------------------------------
- This version's automatic build failed on a Wrecking Ball crash, so no
  installer was made for it. Everything in it is included in v1.0.27.
  Its changes were:
- Hurdle Runner: COINS on the track (+10 m each); the high ones need a jump.
- Free Throw: two makes in a row and the next ball is a gold MONEY BALL
  (double points, +5 seconds).
- Plate Spinner: six saves in a row send a CALM pulse through every plate.
- Grapple Gap: white SLINGSHOT anchors fling you much harder, and a SAFETY NET
  catches one fall (comes back as you progress).
- Skeet Range: GOLD clays count 3 and forgive 2 misses; red DECOYS cost 2
  misses if you shoot them (letting them fall is fine).
- Arena Fighter: land hits to fill a SUPER meter, then press L (or Y on a pad)
  for a super strike that mostly punches through a block.
