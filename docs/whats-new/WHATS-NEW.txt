100 MIMI GAMES - WHAT'S BEEN ADDED
==================================

Newest update first. Every update is also in its own file in the "updates"
folder. Each one is released to the website and the Windows app together
(the app updates itself the next time you open it).

v1.0.47  -  26 Sep 2026
Fix: the flashing school holiday colours now build properly
-----------------------------------------------------------
The previous update (v1.0.46) had a typo in the "What's new" text that stopped the website and the apps from building, so it never went live. This release fixes that and includes the same change: during the school holidays the menu background flashes through every palette colour, one flat colour at a time, in a random order each visit. The "Holiday colours" switch in Settings turns it off.

v1.0.46  -  26 Sep 2026
School holiday colours now flash through the whole palette
----------------------------------------------------------
During the school holidays the menu background is now one flat colour at a time that flashes through every colour in the arcade's palette (cyan, pink, lime, amber, violet, blue and red), in a new random order each time the menu opens.
It changes once a second with a quick fade, so it is lively but not a strobe. The "Holiday colours" switch in Settings turns it off, and "Black sky" still overrides it.
Only the menu changes. Add ?season=holidays to the address to preview it on any day.

v1.0.45  -  26 Sep 2026
School holiday colours on the menu
----------------------------------
During the school holidays the menu's background sky now gets a new random set of colours every time you open it, and a small "School holidays" greeting appears under the title.
There is a new "Holiday colours" switch in Settings to turn it off (then you get the normal rainbow sky). "Black sky" still overrides it.
The dates used are the Victorian school holidays for 2026 and 2027 (listed in one file, src/engine/Seasons.js, so they are easy to change or extend). To preview it on any day, add ?season=holidays to the address.
Only the menu changes. The games themselves look the same.

v1.0.41  -  26 Sep 2026
Search engine setup for the website
-----------------------------------
The website now has what search engines need to find and list it: a proper page title and description, share previews (picture and text when the link is posted in chats and social apps), a sitemap.xml, a robots.txt, a canonical address, and structured data that lists all 100 games with their descriptions.
The game list is also written into the page for crawlers that do not run JavaScript, and it is rebuilt automatically from the game list every release.
No change to how anything plays.

v1.0.40  -  25 Sep 2026
Rocoin tools in your profile
----------------------------
In the account window (the button in the menu) there is a new "Rocoin tools" tick-box. Tick it and a password box appears.
With the right password you can give yourself any number of Rocoins, or switch on Infinite Rocoins so every power in every game is free. While infinite is on, the coin button shows an infinity sign.
"Lock again" shuts the tools and turns infinite off; your real balance comes straight back.
The password is not stored in the game (only a scrambled hash of it), and infinite mode is never copied to your account backup. It is a simple gate for personal use, not real security.
Also fixes from the last update: Tightrope and Keepie Uppie now give you a moment to get ready, and a few boards and menus are brighter and better placed.

v1.0.36  -  25 Sep 2026
10 new games (32 to 41)
-----------------------
Puzzle games
- Memory Match - flip cards two at a time to find the eight pairs. Every wrong pair
  costs a second. Your score is your time (lower is better).

- Pac-Cube - eat every pellet in the maze while three ghosts chase you; big pellets

- Flappy Cube - flap through the gaps between the pipes.
- Gravity Flip - race down a corridor, flipping between floor and ceiling to slip
  past the blocks.
- Catch the Stars - slide a basket under falling stars (gold is worth triple) and
  dodge the bombs.
All ten work with mouse or touch, have on-screen touch buttons where they need them,
and each has its own Rocoin challenges (three targets per game).

------------------------------------------------------------------------

v1.0.18  -  24 Sep 2026
Rocoins, powers and challenges
------------------------------
- New currency: Rocoins. The coin button (top bar in every game, and on the
  menu) shows your balance. Press the ` key (under Esc) in any game to open the
  Rocoins panel.
- EARN them by beating challenges: three score targets in every game
  (easy 5, medium 12, hard 30 coins), three new daily challenges (10 each,
  reset at midnight) and one-off badges (finish 1/25/100 runs, play 5/15/all
  games, beat your personal best 5/25 times).
- SPEND them on powers, by button or by typing a command (slowmo, /heal, help,
  coins). Each power costs Rocoins and you can only use what you can afford:
    Slow-mo 15, Wide view 8, Wireframe world 5, Disco colours 4,
    Retro screen 4, Confetti 3.
  Blockcraft: Full heal 10 (own PvP world only). Kart Circuit: Turbo boost 12,
  Free item 6 (offline races only).
- Powers never change a score, so they cannot be used to fake a challenge.
- Signed in, your Rocoins are backed up to your account and follow you to
  other devices (two devices can earn and spend at once and still add up).
  A different account on the same browser gets its own wallet.
