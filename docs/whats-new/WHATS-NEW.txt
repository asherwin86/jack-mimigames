100 MIMI GAMES - WHAT'S BEEN ADDED
==================================

Newest update first. Every update is also in its own file in the "updates"
folder. Each one is released to the website and the Windows app together
(the app updates itself the next time you open it).

v1.0.31  -  25 Sep 2026
Scroll the game list with a controller
--------------------------------------
- Fixed: with a controller you could not scroll down the list of games. Now the
  right stick scrolls it, and so do the D-pad up/down and the two bumpers
  (LB/RB). Pushing the left-stick cursor to the top or bottom edge of the screen
  also keeps scrolling that way.
- Works in the desktop app and the website with a controller too, and in the
  Android app on the Shield TV.

v1.0.30  -  25 Sep 2026
Android app: controllers now work (fix for the Shield TV)
---------------------------------------------------------
- Fixed the game controller doing nothing in the Android app on the Shield TV.
  Android's built-in web view doesn't reliably offer the browser's controller
  support, so the app now reads the controller itself (sticks, triggers, buttons
  and D-pad) and hands it to the games. Kart Circuit gets it too.
- The controller's B button no longer closes the app by mistake.
- Start on a controller now pauses any game (like Escape on a keyboard) - handy
  on a TV with no keyboard. Kart Circuit and Blockcraft keep their own pause.
- Still to confirm on the Shield: I could not test this on the device itself.

v1.0.29  -  25 Sep 2026
Android app (APK) that works offline, with touch controls
---------------------------------------------------------
- New: 100-Mimi-Games.apk - the whole arcade bundled inside an Android app, so
  it runs with no internet. It is attached to each release on GitHub, and a copy
  goes in the "100 mimi games" folder on the E: drive next to the Windows installer.
- Landscape and full-screen. It appears in the Android TV launcher as well as
  on phones and tablets (a controller or TV remote works like on the desktop).
- On touch screens there is now an on-screen thumb-stick and buttons in the
  games that need a keyboard (Cube Dodger, Hurdle Runner, Gem Grab, Snake Cube,
  Frog Hopper, Platform Hop, Lava Floor, Sumo Arena, Marble Maze, Tunnel Run,
  Orbit Dodge, Maze Escape, Grapple Gap, Arena Fighter), four big lane buttons in
  Beat Lanes, and a pause button in every game. Tap-and-drag games work by
  touch as they are. Blockcraft and Kart Circuit keep their own touch controls.
- Things that need the internet (signing in, cloud worlds, multiplayer, Kart
  online) still do; everything else works offline.

v1.0.28  -  25 Sep 2026
New in Orbit Dodge and Blockcraft
---------------------------------
- Orbit Dodge: GOLD stars count 3. White CHRONO stars slow the debris rings to
  about a third for 5 seconds.
- Blockcraft: seven new blocks - Glowstone (always bright; small pockets deep
  underground), Ice, Red Wool, Blue Wool, Yellow Wool, Green Wool and
  Bookshelf.
- The Blockcraft hotbar now has 18 slots. Slots 12-18 have no number key:
  click them or scroll the mouse wheel (or use the controller bumpers).
- The multiplayer server accepts the new blocks. (An old copy of the app that
  has not updated yet can crash opening a world that contains them - the app
  updates itself when restarted.)

v1.0.27  -  25 Sep 2026
Fix: Wrecking Ball crash (plus the v1.0.26 additions)
-----------------------------------------------------
- Fixed Wrecking Ball crashing when a TNT block set off its neighbours in the
  middle of a swing.
- Includes everything listed under v1.0.26 (Hurdle Runner, Free Throw, Plate
  Spinner, Grapple Gap, Skeet Range, Arena Fighter).

v1.0.26  -  25 Sep 2026
(build failed - its changes shipped in v1.0.27)
-----------------------------------------------
- This version's automatic build failed on a Wrecking Ball crash, so no
  installer was made for it. Everything in it is included in v1.0.27.
  Its changes were:
- Hurdle Runner: COINS on the track (+10 m each); the high ones need a jump.
- Free Throw: two makes in a row and the next ball is a gold MONEY BALL
  (double points, +5 seconds).
- Plate Spinner: six saves in a row send a CALM pulse through every plate.
- Grapple Gap: white SLINGSHOT anchors fling you much harder, and a SAFETY NET
  catches one fall (comes back as you progress).
- Skeet Range: GOLD clays count 3 and forgive 2 misses; red DECOYS cost 2
  misses if you shoot them (letting them fall is fine).
- Arena Fighter: land hits to fill a SUPER meter, then press L (or Y on a pad)
  for a super strike that mostly punches through a block.

v1.0.25  -  25 Sep 2026
New in Whack-a-Cube, Fruit Slice, Wrecking Ball, Frog Hopper and Artillery Duel
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Whack-a-Cube: white CLOCK cubes add 4 seconds when smashed.
- Fruit Slice: GOLDEN fruit starts a 6-second frenzy (twice the fruit, twice
  the points). Pink HEART fruit gives a life back (only appears when you are
  missing one).
- Wrecking Ball: red TNT blocks. Knock one loose and it blows up the blocks
  around it.
- Frog Hopper: a golden FLY appears on a safe strip when you have lost a life.
  Catch it to get the life back.
- Artillery Duel: some targets SLIDE side to side (worth 50% more) after your
  first few hits. A bullseye earns a spare shell (up to 15).

v1.0.24  -  25 Sep 2026
New in Beat Lanes, Colour Rush, Lava Floor, Maze Escape and Brick Wall
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Beat Lanes: white STAR notes (every 24th). Hit one for +1 life and 5 seconds
  of double points. Missing a star costs no life.
- Colour Rush: big GOLD rounds (every eighth) pay triple and give a bit more
  time. Every 15 streak wins a life back.
- Lava Floor: blue COOLANT orbs on the tiles. Grab one and the lava drops
  1.8 m.
- Maze Escape: three cyan SHARDS hidden in far rooms. Each takes 4 seconds off.
- Brick Wall: some bricks drop CAPSULES. Green = wide paddle (12 s), cyan =
  slow ball (9 s), pink = extra life.

v1.0.23  -  25 Sep 2026
New in Paddle Rally, Platform Hop, Sumo Arena and Tunnel Run
------------------------------------------------------------
- Paddle Rally: a glowing TARGET moves about the far wall. Bounce the ball off
  it for +2 bonus points; every third hit buys back a lost life. Your score is
  your best rally plus the bonus.
- Platform Hop: cyan SPRINGS on every sixth platform fire you about two floors
  up.
- Sumo Arena: a huge BOSS rolls in every fifth wave. It is heavy and slow to
  turn; shoving it off counts as 3.
- Tunnel Run: fly through the blue ORBS (one in every eighth wall) to slow the
  tunnel to 70% for 4 seconds.

v1.0.22  -  25 Sep 2026
New in Block Stacker, Asteroid Blaster, Marble Maze and Simon Cubes
-------------------------------------------------------------------
- Block Stacker: three PERFECT drops in a row win some width back (up to the
  starting width). There is a Streak counter.
- Asteroid Blaster: glowing rocks now and then. Green = mends one hull point,
  cyan = 7 seconds of rapid fire. They do not split; worth 150.
- Marble Maze: four cyan TIME CRYSTALS on the board. Each takes 2 seconds off
  your clock.
- Simon Cubes: every fifth round you must play the sequence BACKWARDS
  (the round announces it).

v1.0.21  -  25 Sep 2026
New in Cube Dodger, Sky Hoops, Snake Cube and Gem Grab
------------------------------------------------------
- Cube Dodger: SHIELD pickups (cyan ring) soak up one crash and shatter the
  block. STARS add 40 m to your distance.
- Sky Hoops: every seventh ring is a small GOLDEN ring. Harder to thread, but
  it counts as 3 rings and adds 5 seconds.
- Snake Cube: GOLDEN APPLES appear now and then (+3 length, no speed-up, they
  flicker and vanish after 7 seconds). ROCKS start to appear once you have
  eaten 4 apples - hit one and you are out.
- Gem Grab: MAGNET power-up reels in gems for 7 seconds. ICE CRYSTAL freezes
  every drone for 4 seconds (frozen drones cannot hurt you).

v1.0.20  -  25 Sep 2026
Kart Circuit: no more sudden last place
---------------------------------------
- Fixed your position dropping from 1st to last (24th in the big races) for a
  few seconds after every lap, and the same jump at the start of a race.
  Positions now follow how far round the track each racer really is.
- This also fixes the bots' catch-up speed, which used the same wrong number.

v1.0.19  -  25 Sep 2026
Kart Circuit: laps count up
---------------------------
- A Laps counter in the top bar goes up every lap you finish and never resets.
  It is Kart Circuit's arcade score.
- Kart Circuit challenges are now "Complete 8 / 25 / 60 laps".
- Race wins still show the "RACE WON" message.

v1.0.18  -  24 Sep 2026
Rocoins, powers and challenges
------------------------------
- New currency: Rocoins. The coin button (top bar in every game, and on the
  menu) shows your balance. Press the ` key (under Esc) in any game to open the
  Rocoins panel.
- EARN them by beating challenges: three score targets in every game
  (easy 5, medium 12, hard 30 coins), three new daily challenges (10 each,
  reset at midnight) and one-off badges (finish 1/25/100 runs, play 5/15/all
  games, beat your personal best 5/25 times).
- SPEND them on powers, by button or by typing a command (slowmo, /heal, help,
  coins). Each power costs Rocoins and you can only use what you can afford:
    Slow-mo 15, Wide view 8, Wireframe world 5, Disco colours 4,
    Retro screen 4, Confetti 3.
  Blockcraft: Full heal 10 (own PvP world only). Kart Circuit: Turbo boost 12,
  Free item 6 (offline races only).
- Powers never change a score, so they cannot be used to fake a challenge.
- Signed in, your Rocoins are backed up to your account and follow you to
  other devices (two devices can earn and spend at once and still add up).
  A different account on the same browser gets its own wallet.

v1.0.17  -  24 Sep 2026
See which devices you are signed in on
--------------------------------------
- Open your account (the button at the top of the menu). There is now a
  "Signed in on" list: every device with a plain name like "Chrome on
  Windows" or "Desktop app (Windows)", when it signed in and when it was
  last used. The one you are on says "This device".
- Sign any device out, or press "Sign out all other devices". The signed-out
  device notices the next time it talks to the server and shows the sign-in
  form again.
- Up to 10 devices per account; a device not used for 90 days is signed out.
- Signing in now gives each device its own random key instead of keeping a
  hash of your password on the device (safer, and it is what makes remote
  sign-out possible). Existing sign-ins switch over on their own.
